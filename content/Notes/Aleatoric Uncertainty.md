- **Definition:** 
	- Inherent randomness or noise built directly into the data generation process.
    
- **Data Scaling:** 
	- Does **not** decrease as you collect more training data.
    
- **Common Sources:** 
	- Noisy sensors, measurement errors, or fundamentally ambiguous inputs (e.g., highly distorted or overlapping characters).
