The chip industry is generally split into two main business models:

- **Fabless Companies:** 
	- They design the chips (the architecture and logic) but own zero factories. 
	- They send their designs to someone else to build. _Nvidia, AMD, Qualcomm, MediaTek, Novatek, Phison, and Silicon Motion are all Fabless._
    
- **Foundries:** 
	- They don't design chips; they just operate the multi-billion-dollar factories (fabs) that manufacture them. **TSMC** is the undisputed king of foundries.
    
_(Note: **Intel** is historically an IDM—Integrated Device Manufacturer—meaning they traditionally designed and built their own chips, though they are trying to separate these business units now)._
