Think of the **Kronecker Delta** ($\delta_{ki}$) as a mathematical "on/off switch" or an `if-else` statement compressed into a single symbol. It takes two indices (like $k$ and $i$) and evaluates to either $1$ or $0$ based on a simple rule:

$$\delta_{ki} = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } k = i \\ 0 & \text{if } k \neq i \end{cases}$$

If the two subscripts match, it turns "on" ($1$). If they are different, it turns "off" ($0$).
