title: Why do AI companies give their models away for free?

It seems crazy to spend $100 million training an AI model just to release it as "open-weight" for anyone to download. But it is a brilliant business strategy:

- **Free Crowdsourced R&D:** When Meta, Qwen, or DeepSeek release a model, thousands of developers worldwide immediately start fixing bugs, writing optimization code, and finding ways to make it run faster. The company gets millions of dollars worth of free engineering labor.
    
- **Destroying Closed Competitors:** By giving away a model that is 95% as good as a closed, paid model, they aggressively undercut the pricing power of companies like OpenAI or Google.
    
- **Ecosystem Lock-In:** If developers build all their apps, tools, and workflows around a specific open architecture (like Meta's Llama or Nous's Hermes), that company controls the standard for the industry. They then make their money by selling cloud hosting, enterprise support, and massive data infrastructure to corporations.
