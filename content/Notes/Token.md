A token is a fragment of a word. Think of tokens like Lego blocks used to build sentences.

Instead of processing words like "Obsidian" or "notebook," the AI cuts them into smaller pieces.

- A short, common word like `the` or `cat` is usually **1 token**.
    
- A longer or less common word like `Obsidian` might be broken into **2 or 3 tokens** (like `Obs` + `id` + `ian`).
    

> **The Quick Rule of Thumb:** > 100 English words is roughly equal to **130 tokens**.
