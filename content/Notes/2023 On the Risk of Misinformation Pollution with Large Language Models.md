# Abstract
What are the main takeaways, answers to research questions, or new findings?

Reveals that 
- LLMs can act as effective misinformation generators, leading to a significant  degradation (up to 87%) in the performance of  ODQA (Open-Domain Question  Answering) systems.
- disparities in the attributes associated with persuading  humans and machines, presenting an obstacle to current human-centric approaches to combat misinformation.

3 defense strategies: 
1. misinformation detection
2. vigilant prompting
3. reader ensemble
