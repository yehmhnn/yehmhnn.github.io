In statistics, a **point estimate** is a single number used to represent a whole range of possibilities.

- **The Analogy:** If I ask how long it takes you to commute to Heidelberg University, and you say **"25 minutes,"** that is a point estimate.
    
- **The Reality:** In reality, it might take 20 minutes on a good day and 40 minutes if the train is delayed. By giving me just one number (the mean), you are hiding the **uncertainty** and the **variance**.
