The **Data Wall** is the literal point of exhaustion where AI companies run out of high-quality, unique, human-generated text on the internet to satisfy those Scaling Laws.

Because [[Scaling Laws]] demand that data ($D$) increases linearly alongside model size ($N$), the industry has burned through the internet’s text data at an exponential rate.

### The Mechanics of the Bottleneck

- **The Stock:** 
	- High-quality public human text (books, scientific papers, well-written code, Wikipedia, filtered web pages) is finite—estimated to sit somewhere between **100T to 300T tokens**.
    
- **The Consumption:** 
	- By 2024 to 2026, state-of-the-art models (like Llama 3/4 generation architectures) have already been trained on 15T to 30T+ tokens.
    
- **The Crisis:** 
	- We are rapidly approaching the absolute ceiling of usable human text. 
	- Because of copyright lawsuits, paywalls (like Reddit, Twitter/X, and news sites locking down their data), and the sheer volume of low-quality spam or AI-generated garbage flooding the web, **the pool of raw, high-quality human data is dried up.**
